Appendix A: Tutor Read Agreement (Full Text)
Introduction
Purpose
This document specifies the software requirements for TutorConnectPro, a web-based platform that
connects private tutors, parents/students, and tuition centers, with all onboarding, matching, and
verification managed by a central Admin. This SRS is intended to guide design, development, and testing
of the platform.
Scope
TutorConnectPro is a web application (desktop/mobile browser, no native app) with four user types:
Tutor, Parent/Student, Tuition Center, and Admin. The platform facilitates:
Tutor and tuition center onboarding with document verification.
Posting and matching of tuition requirements between parents and tutors.
A demo-class approval workflow before any tutor is assigned.
Commission and subscription fee collection from tutors (screenshot-based payment proof, manually
verified by Admin).
Ongoing tuition management: attendance, monthly student reports, and in-app chat with Admin.
A tuition-center sub-module for managing batches, attendance, performance, and fee reminders
independently of Admin.
Direct monthly tuition-fee payments between parents and tutors, after the first month, are outside the
platform (offline, personal transactions) and are not tracked in the system.

Functional Requirements — Tutor Module
FR-T1: Tutor Registration
The registration form shall capture:
1. Name
2. Age
3. Qualification
4. WhatsApp number
5. Experience
6. Email ID
7. Password
8. Gender
9. Preferred location(s) — multi-select
10. “Can teach up to which class” — selection
11. Teaching mode — Online / Offline / Both
12. Document uploads (for verification):
Aadhar card
Passport-size photo
PAN card (optional/if available)
Memos (educational/experience certificates)
13. Read Agreement — displayed in full, accepted via a mandatory checkbox before submission.
Agreement content includes:
50% commission is payable from the tutor’s first month’s salary.
Tutor must not share student/parent profile information with anyone outside the platform.
Tutor must maintain professional conduct at all times.
Taking on 2 or more tuitions concurrently requires an active subscription.
Subscription pricing: Rs. 300 (3 months) / Rs. 600 (6 months) / Rs. 900 (9 months) / Rs. 1200 (12
months).
No fake registrations permitted.
Validation rules: - Email must be unique across the system. Duplicate email is rejected at registration
(“fake registration” check). - All fields listed above are mandatory except PAN card. - Agreement
checkbox must be checked to enable the Submit button.
Post-submission status: Registration enters a Pending Verification state. Tutor cannot log in to full
features (cannot apply for tuitions) until Admin manually reviews and approves the uploaded documents
and agreement acceptance.

FR-T2: Tutor Login
Login fields: Email ID, Password.
On successful login, tutor is routed to their dashboard, with feature access depending on approval
status (Pending / Approved / Rejected).
FR-T3: Tutor Dashboard — Before Tuition Assignment
1. View Tuition Requirements: Tutor can see a list of all open tuition requirements posted by Admin
(requirement details such as class, subject, location, salary range — as entered by Admin; exact
requirement fields defined in FR-A2).
2. Apply for a Tuition: Tutor can apply to any listed open requirement. No pre-shortlisting restriction
— any approved tutor may apply to any open requirement.
3. Demo Outcome Tracking: After a demo class is conducted (see FR-P4), the tutor’s application
shows a status of Accepted or Rejected, updated by Admin (Admin conveys the parent’s decision
to the tutor; the tutor never receives this decision directly from the parent). If Rejected, the tutor
remains free to apply for other open requirements.
4. Chat with Admin: Tutor has access to a real-time, in-app chat inbox with Admin.
5. Subscribe for 2+ Tuitions: If a tutor wants to apply for a second (or further) concurrent tuition
while already holding one, the system requires an active subscription. The subscription payment
must be completed before the tutor is allowed to apply to the additional (2nd, 3rd, …) tuition
requirement. Subscription plans: Rs. 300/3 months, Rs. 600/6 months, Rs. 900/9 months, Rs.
1200/12 months.
Payment method: platform displays a payment QR/scanner; tutor pays and uploads a
screenshot as proof; Admin manually verifies and marks the subscription as active.
FR-T4: Tutor Dashboard — After Tuition Assignment
1. First-Month Commission Payment (mandatory gate):
Once a tuition is assigned and the tutor has collected the first month’s salary from the parent
(agreed bilaterally, see FR-T4.2), the tutor must pay 50% of that first month’s salary to Admin.
Payment method: platform displays a payment QR/scanner; tutor pays and uploads a
screenshot as proof of payment.
Admin manually verifies the screenshot and marks the payment as Paid.
The system shall clearly display to the tutor: “Pay your first month’s half salary to Admin —
remaining months are yours,” along with the current payment status (Pending Upload / Pending
Verification / Paid).
2. Salary Negotiation: Monthly salary amount is negotiated directly and offline between tutor and
parent; the system does not fix or enforce a salary figure. From the 2nd month onward, all fee
payments happen directly between tutor and parent, offline, with no tracking or involvement by the
platform.
3. Monthly Earnings: Tutor dashboard displays a running record/summary of monthly earnings
entries logged by the tutor (self-reported, since actual payment is offline).
4. Monthly Student Report: Tutor must submit a monthly report per assigned student containing:
Attendance
Syllabus covered
Marks
Remarks
5. Attendance: Tutor self-marks attendance per class/day (simple checkbox-based marking; no
geolocation or photo proof required).
6. Apply for Additional Tuitions: Tutor may continue to browse and apply for other open tuition
requirements even after being assigned one (subject to the subscription rule in FR-T3.5 if this would
be their 2nd+ concurrent tuition).
7. Chat with Admin: Real-time in-app chat remains available.

Functional Requirements — Parent/Student Module
FR-P1: Parent/Student Registration
The registration form shall capture:
1. Student Name
2. Parent Names (Mother and Father, or Guardian)
3. Email ID
4. Password
5. Mobile Number
Validation rules: - Email must be unique across the system. - One parent account may register
multiple children/tuition requests under the same login.
FR-P2: Parent/Student Login
Login fields: Email ID, Password.
FR-P3: Apply for a Tuition (Requirement Posting)
Parent posts a tuition requirement specifying:
1. Name of the student
2. Class
3. School name
4. Syllabus — CBSE / SSC / ICSE (selectable)
This requirement is submitted to Admin, who lists it for tutors to apply to (see FR-A2, FR-T3.1).
FR-P4: Demo and Tutor Assignment Workflow
1. Admin selects a tutor (from applicants) for a parent’s posted requirement and arranges a demo.
2. The demo is conducted by the tutor visiting the student’s house in person; scheduling is tracked in-
app but the demo itself is a manual/offline process.
3. Attending the demo is at the parent’s discretion (parents may or may not personally observe it).
4. After the demo, the parent marks the outcome in-app as Accepted or Rejected for that tutor.
5. Admin reviews the parent’s decision and conveys the outcome to the tutor as Accepted/Rejected
(tutor never sees the parent’s decision directly — always relayed via Admin, per FR-T3.3).
6. If Rejected, Admin may offer the parent another tutor option; the parent may choose among
candidates presented (parent’s choice is not restricted to one option — parent may consider multiple
tutor candidates before finalizing).
7. If Accepted, the tuition is formally assigned to that tutor.
FR-P5: Parent Dashboard — After Tutor Assignment
1. Tutor Details Reveal: Once Admin formally assigns a tutor and the demo is marked Accepted, the
tutor’s contact details become visible to the parent (previously hidden per FR-P6).
2. Student Monthly Report: Parent can view the monthly report submitted by the tutor (attendance,
syllabus covered, marks, remarks — as per FR-T4.4).
3. Monthly Tuition Fee Payment: Handled directly and offline between parent and tutor from the
first month onward; not tracked or processed within the platform.

Functional Requirements — Tuition Center Module
FR-C1: Tuition Center Registration
The registration form shall capture:
1. Principal/Owner Name
2. Email ID
3. Password
4. Documents of the center (for verification — uploaded as files)
Validation rules: - Email must be unique across the system. - Registration enters a Pending
Verification state; Admin manually reviews uploaded documents and approves/rejects the center
account.
FR-C2: Tuition Center Login
Login fields: Email ID, Password.
FR-C3: Post Tutor Requirements to Admin’s Tutor Pool
A tuition center can also request tutors from the same Admin-managed tutor pool used for individual
parent requirements, by specifying:
1. Class
2. Salary
3. Eligibility rules for tutor
4. Location
This follows the same Admin-mediated matching flow described in FR-A2/FR-T3.1 (tutors from the shared
pool can apply; Admin manages the assignment).
FR-C4: Tuition Center Independent Operations
Once Admin approves a tuition center’s registration, the center’s day-to-day operations are managed
independently by the center’s own staff, with no further involvement from Admin. This includes:
1. Select Class: Center manages classes 1–10.
2. Batches: Create and manage batches (a batch is a group of students, per class, taught by the
center’s tutor(s)).
3. Attendance of Students: Center staff mark attendance for students in each batch.
4. Performance Tracking: Record performance of each student.
5. Send Performance to Parents: Center staff can send a student’s performance report to that
student’s parent through the system.
6. Fees: Track fee status per student.
7. Fee Reminders: Center staff manually send fee reminders to parents (no automated SMS/email
trigger).
8. Exam Reports: Record and share exam reports per student.

Cross-Cutting Rules
1. Duplicate Email Prevention: No two accounts (across tutor, parent, or tuition center) may share
the same email address; this is the platform’s fake-registration check.
2. Visibility Gating: Contact details between tutors and parents are mutually hidden until Admin
formally assigns and the demo is Accepted (applies both directions — see FR-T5 and FR-P6).
3. Manual Verification Everywhere: All document verification (tutor KYC, tuition center documents)
and all payment verification (commission, subscription) is performed manually by Admin — no OCR,
no automated payment gateway.
4. In-App Chat vs. WhatsApp: The home page WhatsApp button and the logged-in real-time chat
inbox are two separate, independent communication channels.
5. Offline Financial Transactions: Monthly tuition fees (parent → tutor, from month 1 onward) are
personal/offline transactions and are outside platform scope, except for the first-month 50%
commission (tutor → Admin) which is tracked in-app via screenshot upload and manual verification.
Non-Functional Requirements
NFR1: Platform
Web application only (responsive design for desktop and mobile browsers); no native mobile app in
current scope.
NFR2: Technology Stack
Database: MongoDB.
Given the requirement to “keep it easy” alongside MongoDB, the recommended stack is the MERN
stack: MongoDB (database), Express.js (backend framework), React.js (frontend), Node.js (runtime)
— chosen for ease of development, a single JavaScript language across the stack, and natural fit
with MongoDB’s document model (useful for variable-structure data such as tutor documents, chat
messages, and monthly reports).
NFR3: Security
Passwords stored using industry-standard hashing (e.g., bcrypt).
Role-based access control enforced for all four user types.
Uploaded documents (Aadhar, PAN, photos, memos, payment screenshots) stored securely with
access restricted to Admin and the uploading user.
NFR4: Usability
Simple, form-driven registration flows suitable for non-technical users (parents, tutors).
Clear status indicators for pending/approved/rejected states (registration, demo, payments).
NFR5: Availability & Performance
Real-time chat should reflect new messages without requiring a full page reload.
Standard web application uptime and responsiveness expectations for a moderate-traffic regional
platform.

NFR6: Data Retention
Uploaded verification documents and payment screenshots retained for audit purposes.
Assumptions and Constraints
1. Admin account(s) are created directly in the system/database (no public admin self-registration
flow).
2. Tuition requirement fields visible to tutors before applying (class, subject, location, salary range,
syllabus) are entered by Admin at the time of listing, based on the parent’s or tuition center’s
original request.
3. “Monthly earnings” reported on the tutor dashboard is a self-reported record for the tutor’s own
reference, since actual monthly fee collection after month 1 is offline and untracked.
4. Tuition center’s internal batch/attendance/performance/fee data is isolated to that center’s own
module and does not feed into Admin’s platform-wide “Monthly Earnings” figure (FR-A6), which
reflects commission and subscription income only.
5. No payment gateway (Razorpay, UPI API, etc.) integration is in scope; all payments are proof-of-
payment (screenshot) plus manual Admin verification.
